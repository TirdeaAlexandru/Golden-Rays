Out with the camera

Photography happens
out in the world.

We learn by looking, trying, and making things together.

In the workshop

We try things
and see what happens.

Camera exercises and conversations with visiting photographers help us find our own way with light, focus, and composition.

Out in Galați

The city gives us
plenty to look at.

Photo walks and school trips take us to new streets, changing light, and views we might have hurried past.

Around the table

We share what
we notice.

We look through each other’s photographs, trade ideas, and learn from the choices behind every frame.

Sometimes, film

A story can move
beyond one frame.

Members work together on short films and submit work to competitions such as FILMMIC and Boovie.

Keep looking

The city is
full of frames.
